Build fitted wardrobes in two bedrooms

Posted on 29 July 2026

Two fitted wardrobes, on the walls shown in the model views. The schematics are the reference for carcass sizes, bay widths and internal layout.

1 / 2

1 / 2

On the ground floor the top 50 cm is a separate run of top boxes: its own carcasses, built and set on top of a 200 cm main run, not one tall carcass divided by a shelf. Each bay's top box takes its own pair of short doors. It carries the same 65 cm depth as the run below and sits flush at the front, with a rail between the two runs.

The first-floor wardrobe has no top boxes. Its doors run full height over the whole 207 cm, with the upper shelf compartments behind them.

Every compartment sits behind doors. No niche or shelf is open to the room.

Two labels on the schematics are misleading and do not override the written scope:

  • “Open storage” is a shelf compartment behind doors, not an open niche.
  • “Adjustable shelves” are fixed, built into the carcass. Nothing in either wardrobe is adjustable.

What the schematics do not show:

  • Compartment heights add up to the external carcass height. Carcass, shelf, plinth and top-rail thicknesses come out of those figures, not on top of them.
  • The first-floor bays are not identical inside. The middle bay drops the second shelf compartment so its hanging space runs higher. Everything below that lines up across all three.

Both wardrobes are to be lit inside, with the wiring route planned before the carcasses are built.

  • Warm white LED strip down both side panels of every bay, out of direct view. The ground-floor top boxes are lit as well, each on its own strip.
  • Door-activated switching, so a bay lights when its door opens and a top box lights on its own doors.
  • Drivers and cabling concealed in the carcass. No visible trunking.
  • A concealed fused spur, not a trailing plug lead.

Materials:

  • MDF for every door and drawer front, not MFC.
  • MFC for carcasses, shelves and drawer boxes, with matching ABS edging on visible edges.
  • MDF also for plinths, back panels and scribe fillets.
  • Matt throughout. No gloss, no woodgrain, no patterned decor.
  • Fixed shelves, built into the carcass. Nothing adjustable.

Every door and drawer front is sprayed paint on 22 mm MDF, hard-wearing enough for daily handling. Wrapped fronts are not acceptable: no vinyl wrap, foil wrap or thermofoil, and no laminated or melamine-faced door, whatever it is called in the quotation. This is a hard constraint, not a preference to be priced against — summers here now get hot enough to lift and delaminate a wrapped front.

Painted MDF and MFC will not match by being ordered to the same colour name. Door and carcass samples must be approved side by side before anything is ordered. End panels, plinth and top rail follow the doors, not the carcass interiors.

One colour per wardrobe, to be settled against a RAL or paint-brand reference. Take the colour from these values, not from the renders.

  • Buckingham Green #014137 Ground-floor wardrobe.
  • Garnet red #733635 First-floor wardrobe.

Style is simple Scandinavian: quiet, solid and unfussy.

  • Shaker doors and drawer fronts throughout: a plain frame around a recessed flat panel. No flat slab fronts, no flush trimless detailing.
  • The tall doors are panelled differently on each wardrobe. Ground floor: two panels, a tall one over a short one. First floor: one full-height panel, with no cross rail. The ground-floor top-box doors take a single panel each.
  • Slim plain handles, bar or tubular, one finish throughout.
  • Hinged doors, two to a bay. On the ground floor the top boxes take their own shorter pair of doors per bay; on the first floor the doors run full height with no separate upper doors.
  • Soft-close hinges and drawer runners throughout.
  • Soft-push opening on every door and every drawer, on both wardrobes. Handles are fitted as well as the push mechanism, not in place of it.
  • Scribed to walls, floor and ceiling. Assume both rooms are out of true.

On the first-floor wardrobe, both doors of bay B3 — the right-hand bay as the room sees it — are mirrored: one unbroken full-height mirror per door, in place of the flat panel. Safety-backed, held by the shaker frame rather than fixed on afterwards.

Materials

  • 22 mm MDF for every shaker door and drawer front
  • Hard-wearing spray finish in the chosen colour
  • MFC panels for the ground-floor carcasses in the nearest matt Buckingham Green decor
  • MFC panels for the first-floor carcasses in the nearest matt garnet red decor
  • Matching ABS edging
  • MDF for plinths, back panels and scribe fillets
  • Hanging rails and end supports
  • Soft-close hinges and drawer runners
  • Soft-push latches for every door and drawer
  • Safety-backed mirror panels, one full-height panel per door, for both doors of first-floor bay B3
  • Slim bar handles in a single finish
  • Warm white LED strips, drivers and door switches
