Make balustrade and stair edges safe

Posted on 22 July 2026

The short ground-floor stair has sharp square timber corners on its exposed edges, posts and post caps, at a height and position where people brush past them.

1 / 3

Round all of them off permanently, then sand smooth and refinish to match the existing wood. The work covers:

  • The stair edges and nosings.
  • The balustrade posts.
  • The post caps.

The balustrade structure must not be weakened or altered. The corner radius has to stop short of the load-bearing section of any component.

Materials

  • Round-over router bit or shaping tools
  • Graded sandpaper
  • Matching wood finish
  • Wood filler, if needed
