Install mirrored storage cabinets in both bathrooms

Posted on 22 July 2026

Install a customer-supplied mirrored storage cabinet above the sink in each bathroom. The ground-floor bathroom wall is tiled, so use suitable tile bits, check for concealed services and seal all penetrations properly.

Materials

  • Two customer-supplied mirrored cabinets
  • Tile and masonry drill bits
  • Suitable wall anchors and fixings
  • Sanitary sealant
DIY fallback Limited

The drilling and fixing are DIY-capable, but the mirrored cabinets must not be lifted or positioned alone. Wall construction, cabinet weight and concealed services have to determine the fixings.

Fallback approach

  • Confirm each cabinet's weight, fixing points and intended height, then identify the wall construction.
  • Scan the whole fixing area and mark a level line with the cabinet still safely on the floor.
  • Use a second physically capable adult to support the cabinet while positions are checked and during final mounting.
  • For the tiled wall, drill the tile slowly without hammer mode, switch to the substrate-appropriate method behind it, and seal penetrations.

Tools and supplies

  • Wall detector
  • Tape measure, spirit level or cross-line laser
  • Hard-ceramic tile bits and masonry bits
  • Drill-driver
  • Fixings rated for the wall and loaded cabinet
  • Sanitary silicone, eye protection and a second adult

Stop and hand over if

  • The wall construction or load capacity cannot be confirmed.
  • The scan suggests concealed wiring or plumbing near a fixing point.
  • The cabinet cannot be supported continuously by a second capable person.
  • A tile begins to crack or the fixing will not tighten securely.
