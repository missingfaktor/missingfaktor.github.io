Create washer-dryer access hole

Posted on 22 July 2026

Drill a suitably sized access hole through the wooden cabinet at the height required by the new washer-dryer’s hoses and power cable. Check for hidden services first and leave all cut edges smooth and protected.

Materials

  • Appropriate hole saw or wood drill bit
  • Edge trim or grommet, if required
