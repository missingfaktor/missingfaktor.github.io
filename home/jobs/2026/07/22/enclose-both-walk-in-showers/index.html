Replace both walk-in shower enclosures

Posted on 22 July 2026

Both showers are walk-in with open entrances, so water escapes onto the bathroom floor. Each needs a complete replacement enclosure that contains the water without spoiling the bathroom’s finish.

1 / 2

Full replacement of both enclosures is the only viable route; the existing glasswork cannot be adapted with added doors or return panels.

Each replacement enclosure must:

  • Fit the measured dimensions of the existing shower area and tray.
  • Fully enclose the shower entrance so water no longer escapes onto the floor.
  • Use chrome hardware in one bathroom and brushed brass hardware in the other.

The supplied downstairs shower sketch records:

  • A footprint of 0.80 m wide × 1.09 m long.
  • An existing enclosure height of 1.87 m.
  • A floor-to-ceiling height of 2.27 m.
  • A bottom white section measuring 0.32 m along the dimension shown.

The upstairs shower is expected to share the same other dimensions, but the length of its bottom white section is still to be supplied. All dimensions need confirming before either enclosure is ordered.

Product selection is part of the plumber’s scope: measure both showers and recommend suitable complete enclosures. The recommended products will then be ordered for installation.

Materials

  • Complete chrome-finished shower enclosure, with product and size to be confirmed after measuring
  • Complete brushed-brass-finished shower enclosure, with product and size to be confirmed after measuring
  • Manufacturer-specified installation components and seals
  • Sanitary silicone
