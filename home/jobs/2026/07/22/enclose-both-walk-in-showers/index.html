Enclose both walk-in showers

Posted on 22 July 2026

Both showers are walk-in with open entrances, so water escapes onto the bathroom floor. Each needs enclosing without spoiling the finish it already has.

1 / 2

Two routes, in order of preference:

  1. Add doors or return panels to the existing screens and trays, if compatible parts can be found.
  2. Replace the glasswork with correctly sized full enclosures if they cannot.

Either way the hardware has to match what is already there: chrome in one bathroom, brushed brass in the other.

Materials

  • Compatible shower doors or return panels
  • Profiles, hinges and support bars
  • Water seals
  • Wet-area fixings
  • Sanitary silicone
