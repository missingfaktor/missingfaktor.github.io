Install bifold-door magnetic hold-open stops

Posted on 22 July 2026

The opening leaf of the bifold door can move and slam in strong winds, and is currently held back with loose bricks. The previously bought top-mounted item is a uPVC window stay, not hardware designed for the weight of a bifold door, and will be returned rather than fitted. The replacement is a matched pair of purpose-made magnetic patio-door stops, providing a buffer and a hold-open in calm conditions.

Ordered hardware:

  • Two Doorstop World 150mm Patio Doorstops, 90°, Black, With Magnetic Hold, with the Normal door screw option. The dispatch reference identifies the pair as 2-90A.
  • Each stop is supplied with its rubber magnetic stop and bracket, two bracket screws, one 25mm stainless-steel screw for the door-side unit, and an anti-rotation grub screw.
  • Fit one stop at the top and one at the bottom, aligned so they share the wind load without twisting the door.
  • Product: https://doorstopworld.com/shop/150mm-patio-doorstop/

Positioning constraints:

  • Hold each 90° bracket against the wall and choose a position where the door meets the rubber stop and magnet square-on, not at an angle.
  • Do not move a stop out of square to gain another 25mm of opening. The supplier warns that angled contact reduces magnetic strength and can cause failure or damage.
  • Confirm solid substrate behind the render for both brackets and a manufacturer-safe fixing zone for each door-side unit on the aluminium leaf.
  • Keep the two contact points aligned so the pair holds the leaf without twisting it.
  • The magnetic hold is intended for calm conditions, not as a storm restraint. Do not leave the door relying on the magnets in high wind.

Supplier fitting sequence for each stop:

  1. On a calm day, have a second adult control the door. Hold the bracket in its final 90° position, bring the door squarely onto the magnet, and mark the first bracket hole with a pencil or pen.
  2. Remove the magnetic unit from the stop and keep it safely attached to another metal object. Keep the strong magnets under control so they cannot snap together, chip or pinch fingers. Do not use the drill through the bracket as a marker: it can damage the protective coating.
  3. Drill the first wall hole with a 7mm masonry bit and fit the brown wall plug. Use an 8mm hole only where the selected plug or fixing requires it.
  4. Tighten the first bracket screw only enough to retain adjustment. Swing the bracket aside, or remove it, before drilling the second hole; brush away the brick dust, then fasten both screws with an 8mm socket or spanner.
  5. Centre the door-side backplate on the rubber stop, peel the backing from its double-sided tape, bring the door squarely onto the stop, and apply moderate pressure so the backplate stays in the correct place on the door.
  6. Unscrew the magnetic body from the taped backplate. On the hard aluminium frame, drill a 3mm pilot hole, then fit the supplied 25mm stainless-steel screw with a hand screwdriver.
  7. Fit and tighten the small grub screw so the backplate cannot rotate, screw the magnetic body back onto it, and test that both stops engage squarely and release cleanly.

Materials

  • Ordered: two black Doorstop World 150mm Patio Doorstops, 90°, with magnetic hold and the Normal door screw option
  • Per stop: supplied rubber magnetic stop and bracket, two bracket screws, adhesive positioning disc, 25mm stainless-steel screw, and anti-rotation grub screw
  • Brown wall plugs matched to a 7mm masonry bit, or alternative exterior-grade plugs and fixings suited to the substrate behind the render
DIY fallback Limited

The ordered pair can be fitted as DIY only after safe fixing points are confirmed at both the wall and the aluminium leaf. The door is heavy and wind-loaded, so this is not a trial-and-error drilling job.

Fallback approach

  • Confirm solid substrate behind the render and a manufacturer-safe fixing zone on the aluminium before drilling.
  • On a calm day, have a second adult control the door while each 90° bracket is positioned for square contact and marked with a pencil or pen.
  • Fit each bracket one hole at a time, moving it clear before drilling so the protective coating is not damaged.
  • Use the double-sided tape to transfer each centred backplate position to the door, then remove the magnetic body to expose the fixing hole.
  • Drill the 3mm metal pilot hole, fit the 25mm stainless-steel screw by hand, tighten the anti-rotation grub screw, and reassemble the magnetic body.
  • Test both stops gently for square engagement, equal loading and clean release before exposing the door to wind.

Tools and supplies

  • Tape measure, level, pencil or pen
  • Wall detector
  • Combi drill with 7mm masonry bit and 3mm metal pilot bit
  • 8mm socket or spanner
  • Hand screwdriver
  • Brown wall plugs or exterior-grade alternatives matched to the substrate
  • A second adult to control the door

Stop and hand over if

  • The substrate behind the render or the construction of the door leaf is uncertain.
  • Either stop cannot meet the door squarely, or the two contact points cannot be aligned without twisting the leaf.
  • The proposed magnet fixing would enter glass, a seal, a drainage channel, a multipoint mechanism or a thermally broken section.
  • The door cannot be held securely and without strain while it is marked and fitted.
