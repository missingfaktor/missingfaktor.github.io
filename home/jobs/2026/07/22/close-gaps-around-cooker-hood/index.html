Close gaps around cooker hood

Posted on 22 July 2026

The cooker hood sits in a cabinet opening much wider than itself, leaving a large open void on each side. Close both neatly with moisture- and heat-resistant infill pieces.

1 / 3

The infill must preserve everything the appliance requires:

  • Ventilation and the duct route.
  • Filter removal.
  • Service access to the hood.
  • The manufacturer's heat clearances.

Materials

  • Aluminium sheet or sealed moisture-resistant board
  • Suitable fixings
  • Heat-resistant sealant
DIY fallback Limited

A removable board infill is a plausible DIY option once the hood's ventilation, heat-clearance and service requirements are known. Aluminium fabrication, or any alteration to the hood, wiring or ducting, is outside this fallback.

Fallback approach

  • Identify the hood model and read its installation instructions before covering either void.
  • Make card templates for both openings and confirm that the filters, ducting and fixings remain accessible.
  • Cut sealed moisture-resistant board while it is clamped to a bench, finish the edges, and use removable mechanical fixings.
  • Run the hood through all settings and check that nothing vibrates, heats up or obstructs airflow.

Tools and supplies

  • Tape measure and combination square
  • Clamps
  • Oscillating multi-tool or jigsaw
  • Files and sanding block
  • Drill-driver and suitable bits
  • Dust extraction, eye protection and hearing protection

Stop and hand over if

  • The model or required clearances cannot be established.
  • A cable, electrical enclosure or part of the duct route would be covered or disturbed.
  • The chosen design needs metal cutting or appliance modification beyond straightforward removable panels.
