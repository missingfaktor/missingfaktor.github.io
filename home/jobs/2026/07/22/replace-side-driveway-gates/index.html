Repair or replace iron side-driveway gates

Posted on 22 July 2026

The existing iron driveway gates have three problems:

  • They are low and climbable.
  • There is corrosion and vertical misalignment around the central latch.
  • The lever hardware appears to be custom-made as part of the gates, rather than a standard locksmith-supplied component.

1 / 2

This work is parked for now. Because the lever looks made-to-order rather than replaceable off the shelf, resuming it means asking a blacksmith or gate fabricator to assess whether the lever can be reproduced, and whether repairing the existing gates or replacing them entirely is the better approach.
