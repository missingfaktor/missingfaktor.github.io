Deep-clean textured ground floor

Posted on 22 July 2026

The ground floor is a textured laminate or LVT-style covering whose pronounced grain traps dirt in the grooves, where ordinary mopping does not reach it.

Deep-clean it with a pH-neutral cleaner and a low-moisture method, working the grooves in particular. The floor must not be:

  • Sanded, oiled, waxed or lacquered.
  • Flooded or left with standing water in the grain.

Materials

  • Laminate or LVT-compatible pH-neutral cleaner
  • Microfibre mop
  • Soft brushes
  • Drying cloths
DIY fallback Reasonable

This is well suited to DIY provided the floor is treated as laminate or LVT until its exact construction is confirmed, and moisture is kept to a minimum.

Fallback approach

  • Vacuum loose grit first, including the textured grooves.
  • Test a diluted pH-neutral cleaner in an inconspicuous area and let it dry completely.
  • Work in small sections with a damp microfibre mop and soft nylon brush rather than flooding the surface.
  • Lift dirty liquid promptly with cloths or a wet-and-dry vacuum, then dry each section.

Tools and supplies

  • Microfibre flat mop
  • Soft nylon detailing brushes
  • Spray bottle
  • Drying cloths
  • Vacuum or wet-and-dry vacuum
  • pH-neutral floor cleaner

Stop and hand over if

  • The test area changes colour, loses sheen, softens or swells.
  • Water is entering joints or remaining trapped in the grooves.
  • The dirt proves to be damage or finish failure rather than removable residue.
