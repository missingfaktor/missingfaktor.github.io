Wall-mount 50-inch living-room TV

Posted on 22 July 2026

The Sony BRAVIA KD-50X75WL 50-inch television is not yet mounted. It needs to go on the living-room wall at an agreed height and position, with the power and signal cables concealed or neatly routed.

The bracket is a One For All WM4421 tilting wall mount. It covers 32 to 65-inch screens, is rated to 100 kg, tilts through 15 degrees, accepts VESA patterns from 100x100 up to 400x400, and holds the screen 25 mm from the wall.

Check the television's actual VESA hole spacing against the bracket before drilling, and confirm that the cables and connectors clear the wall at the 25 mm standoff. The wall construction is not known. The bracket comes with screws and concrete anchors, so a wall that is not solid masonry will need different fixings.

Materials

  • One For All WM4421 tilting wall bracket
  • Wall-specific fixings, if the supplied concrete anchors do not suit the substrate
  • Cable-management materials
DIY fallback Limited

The bracket can be fitted as DIY after the wall construction and loading are confirmed, but the television must not be lifted or mounted alone. Surface cable management is the fallback unless a separate safe in-wall route is designed.

Fallback approach

  • Match the television's VESA hole spacing to the WM4421 wall plate, fit the vertical arms to the back of the screen, and agree the screen centre and viewing height.
  • Identify the wall construction, scan the full fixing area, and select fixings for the actual substrate rather than defaulting to the concrete anchors in the bracket box.
  • Mark and fit the wall plate level, tighten it to the manufacturer's instructions, and load-check it before bringing the television to the wall.
  • Use a second physically capable adult to lift and locate the television, then route power and signal cables in surface trunking.

Tools and supplies

  • Wall detector
  • Tape measure, spirit level or cross-line laser
  • Combi or SDS drill with substrate-appropriate bits
  • Socket set and torque guidance from the bracket manufacturer
  • Dust extraction and eye protection
  • A second physically capable adult

Stop and hand over if

  • The wall construction, fixing position or safe load cannot be confirmed.
  • The scan suggests concealed wiring, pipes or metalwork at a fixing point.
  • The television cannot be lifted and supported by two capable people.
  • The desired cable route would conceal a flexible mains lead inside the wall or require electrical alteration.
