Install SimpliSafe outdoor camera

Posted on 31 July 2026

The SimpliSafe Outdoor Security Camera Series 2 has been bought but is not yet installed. It goes on the Permanent Mount for Outdoor Camera, bought alongside it, on the rear wall above the bifold doors — 0.3 m in from the left-hand corner and 2.4 m above the patio, angled downwards.

1 / 2

The wall is rendered, so the fixings have to reach whatever lies behind it rather than holding in render alone. A rainwater downpipe runs down the corner, and the bracket has to clear it at the marked position.

The camera's aim is agreed on site. Check it in Live View from the mounting point and confirm it before any hole is marked. Ground beyond the property boundary should be kept out of frame as far as is reasonably possible.

Confirm at the mounting point before drilling:

  • A reliable Wi-Fi signal and a stable Live View.
  • No glare, washout from exterior lights, or obstruction in the agreed view.
  • What is behind the render, so the screws hold in something solid rather than in the render alone.
  • That the camera stays reachable by ladder, since its battery has to be recharged periodically.

Materials

  • SimpliSafe Outdoor Security Camera Series 2, already on site
  • SimpliSafe Permanent Mount for Outdoor Camera, bought separately and already on site
  • Outdoor screws and wall plugs to suit whatever is behind the render
  • Outdoor sealant for the drilled holes
DIY fallback Limited

Charging, pairing and checking the view are straightforward to do beforehand. The permanent installation is not, because it means drilling a rendered exterior wall from a ladder at 2.4 m.

Fallback approach

  • Fully charge and pair the camera in advance.
  • Use Live View from roughly the marked point to settle on the view the finished mounting has to give.
  • Clear the ground below the mounting point so a ladder can stand safely.
  • Keep the camera, the permanent mount and all the hardware that came with them together.

Tools and supplies

  • Stable ladder suitable for the working height
  • Combi or SDS drill with masonry bits to suit the wall
  • Wall or service detector
  • Tape measure and spirit level
  • Outdoor fixings and sealant
  • Eye protection and a second adult for ladder safety

Stop and hand over if

  • Wi-Fi or Live View is unreliable at the marked point.
  • What is behind the render cannot be confirmed, so the screws might only hold in the render.
  • A scan suggests concealed wiring, pipework or metalwork at a fixing point.
  • The ladder cannot stand securely on clear, level ground.
  • The agreed view cannot be achieved without recording significant ground beyond the property boundary.
