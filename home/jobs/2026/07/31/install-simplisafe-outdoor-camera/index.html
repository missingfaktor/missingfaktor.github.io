Install SimpliSafe outdoor camera

Posted on 31 July 2026

The SimpliSafe Outdoor Security Camera Series 2 has been bought but is not yet installed. It needs to go on its permanent bracket at high level on the rear elevation, roughly 2 to 2.4 m above the patio and angled downwards by around 30 degrees.

The exact point and the camera's aim are agreed on site. Both are checked in Live View from the proposed position and confirmed before any hole is marked. Ground beyond the property boundary should be kept out of frame as far as is reasonably possible.

Confirm at the proposed position before drilling:

  • A reliable Wi-Fi signal and a stable Live View.
  • No glare, washout from exterior lights, or obstruction in the agreed view.
  • The wall construction behind the exterior finish, so that fixings suit the actual substrate rather than defaulting to those supplied.
  • That the camera stays reachable by ladder, since its battery has to be recharged periodically.

Materials

  • SimpliSafe Outdoor Security Camera Series 2, already on site
  • SimpliSafe permanent outdoor-camera mount, already on site
  • Exterior-grade screws and wall plugs suitable for the actual substrate
  • Exterior sealant for drilled penetrations
DIY fallback Limited

Charging, pairing and checking the view are straightforward to do beforehand. The permanent installation is not, because it means drilling an exterior wall from a ladder at around 2 to 2.4 m.

Fallback approach

  • Fully charge and pair the camera in advance.
  • Use Live View from roughly the proposed position to settle on the view the finished mounting has to give.
  • Clear the ground below the mounting point so a ladder can stand safely.
  • Keep the camera, the permanent mount and all supplied hardware together.

Tools and supplies

  • Stable ladder suitable for the working height
  • Combi or SDS drill with substrate-appropriate masonry bits
  • Wall or service detector
  • Tape measure and spirit level
  • Exterior-grade fixings and sealant
  • Eye protection and a second adult for ladder safety

Stop and hand over if

  • Wi-Fi or Live View is unreliable at the proposed position.
  • The substrate behind the render or exterior finish cannot be confirmed.
  • A scan suggests concealed wiring, pipework or metalwork at a fixing point.
  • The ladder cannot stand securely on clear, level ground.
  • The agreed view cannot be achieved without recording significant ground beyond the property boundary.
