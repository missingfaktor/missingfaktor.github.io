Install SimpliSafe outdoor camera, bell box and video doorbell

Posted on 31 July 2026

Three SimpliSafe devices are on site and none of them is installed: the Outdoor Security Camera Series 2 with its permanent mount, the bell box, and the Video Doorbell Pro with an OhmKat plug-in power supply and wireless chime. The camera goes on the rear wall; the bell box and doorbell go on the front.

Every mounting point is rendered wall. The fixings have to reach whatever lies behind the render rather than holding in render alone, and that has to be established at each point before a hole is marked. The camera and the bell box both sit above head height, so both need a ladder standing on clear, level ground.

The outdoor camera. It goes on its permanent mount on the rear wall above the bifold doors — 0.3 m in from the left-hand corner and 2.4 m above the patio, angled downwards.

1 / 2

The camera's aim is agreed on site. Check it in Live View from the mounting point and confirm it before any hole is marked. Ground beyond the property boundary should be kept out of frame as far as is reasonably possible.

Confirm at the camera position before drilling:

  • A reliable Wi-Fi signal and a stable Live View.
  • No glare, washout from exterior lights, or obstruction in the agreed view.
  • That the bracket clears the downpipe at the marked point.
  • That the camera stays reachable by ladder, since its battery has to be recharged periodically.

The bell box. It wants to be seen from the driveway before anyone reaches the door, but the obvious spot above the front door is not available. The roof comes down to just above the door and window frames, leaving under 100 mm of bare wall below the gutter along the whole front.

1 / 2

The widest clear wall is the strip between the left-hand window and the door, which runs the full height. A bell box there sits at about 2.1 m — seen easily from the driveway, but within reach from the ground.

Settle the bell box before anything is drilled:

  • Whether 2.1 m on the front is good enough, or a higher spot on a side wall is worth having instead.
  • That the box and its plate fit that strip, which is narrow and already carries the house number plaque, and clear the gutter and downpipes.
  • How the box comes off its plate for battery changes. If it slides upward, the gutter directly above may leave too little room.
  • That nothing runs behind the screw positions. A utility cabinet sits at the foot of the same strip with two conduits dropping from it.

Pair the bell box and check that it sounds before it goes up. Once fixed, test that it sounds, sits firmly and reports back to the system.

The doorbell. It goes beside the front door, where it can see a visitor's face. A lamp is already fitted to the right of the door and stays where it is, so the doorbell works around it. It does mean the doorway is lit after dark, which is what the doorbell camera will be relying on at night.

1 / 2

Power does not come from the house wiring. The OhmKat supply plugs into an indoor socket and feeds the doorbell along a 5.5 m low-voltage cable; its wireless chime plugs into another socket indoors. Nothing connects to the mains or to an existing doorbell circuit.

Settle the doorbell before anything is drilled:

  • Which side of the door gives the clearest view of someone at the door and of the approach from the driveway, working around the lamp.
  • That the lamp does not glare into the lens or throw the visitor's face into shadow at night.
  • Whether the doorbell needs a wedge to face the right way, and whether one came in the box.
  • Which indoor socket feeds it, and whether the 5.5 m cable reaches from there without an extension.
  • How the cable gets through the wall without being trapped by the door, and how that hole is sealed.

Clip the doorbell cable or run it in trunking rather than leaving it loose. How the OhmKat transmitter joins the arrangement comes from its own instructions, not from guesswork on the day.

Before the installer leaves: camera fixed and giving the agreed view, bell box firm and level, doorbell powered and answering a press, every drilled hole sealed and the cable secure.

Materials

  • SimpliSafe Outdoor Security Camera Series 2, already on site
  • SimpliSafe Permanent Mount for Outdoor Camera, bought separately and already on site
  • SimpliSafe bell box, already on site
  • SimpliSafe Video Doorbell Pro, already on site
  • OhmKat plug-in power supply with a 5.5 m cable, already on site
  • OhmKat wireless chime transmitter and receiver, already on site
  • Whatever mounting plates and fixings came in the bell box and doorbell boxes; no separate mounts were bought for those two
  • Outdoor screws and wall plugs to suit whatever is behind the render
  • Exterior cable clips or narrow trunking
  • Outdoor sealant for the drilled holes
DIY fallback Limited

Charging, pairing, setting the devices up in the app and settling the views are all straightforward to do beforehand. Fixing them to the wall is not, because it means drilling rendered exterior walls from a ladder and taking a cable through the wall.

Fallback approach

  • Fully charge and pair the camera.
  • Use Live View from roughly the marked rear point to settle on the view the finished mounting has to give.
  • Pair the bell box with the system and confirm at ground level that it sounds.
  • Settle on the bell box position, given how little bare wall the roofline leaves.
  • Pick the indoor socket for the OhmKat supply and check that the 5.5 m cable reaches the intended doorbell position.
  • Add the doorbell in the app, connect it to the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, and check the view in daylight and after dark.
  • Pair the wireless chime and find a socket where it can be heard from the main rooms.
  • Clear the patio and the driveway so a ladder can stand safely at both mounting points.
  • Keep each device together with the mount and hardware that came with it.

Tools and supplies

  • Stable ladder suitable for the working height
  • Combi or SDS drill with masonry bits to suit the wall
  • Wall or service detector
  • Tape measure and spirit level
  • Outdoor fixings, cable clips and sealant
  • Eye and hearing protection and a second adult for ladder safety

Stop and hand over if

  • Wi-Fi or Live View is unreliable at the camera position.
  • The bell box will not pair or sound correctly before it is fitted.
  • What is behind the render cannot be confirmed, so the screws might only hold in the render.
  • A scan suggests concealed wiring, pipework or metalwork at a fixing point.
  • The only workable spot for the bell box fouls the gutter or a downpipe, or leaves no room to take the box off for new batteries.
  • The 5.5 m cable will not reach, or there is no tidy, weatherproof way to bring it through the wall.
  • The agreed camera view cannot be achieved without recording significant ground beyond the property boundary.
  • The ladder cannot stand securely on clear, level ground.
