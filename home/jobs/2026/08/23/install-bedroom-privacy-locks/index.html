Install privacy locks on both bedroom doors

Posted on 23 August 2026

Neither bedroom door can currently be locked by the occupant for privacy. The requirement is an internal privacy lock, not a keyed security lock: each door should lock from inside without a key while remaining releasable from the hallway in an emergency.

Fit a compatible bathroom-style turn-and-release privacy lock to each door, providing:

  • An internal thumbturn that is easy to operate from inside the bedroom.
  • An external emergency release, operable with a coin or flat screwdriver rather than a key.
  • A bathroom mortice lock or integrated privacy latch compatible with the existing handles, door thickness and backset.
  • Visible furniture that matches the existing finish as closely as practical, with any redundant holes or finish damage made good.

Confirm the construction of both doors before ordering or cutting. If either is a fire door, the chosen hardware and installation must preserve its fire performance. Test the thumbturn and the outside emergency release with each door closed before the job is signed off.

Materials

  • Two compatible bathroom mortice locks or integrated privacy latches
  • Matching turn-and-release furniture, if separate from the chosen lock cases
  • Matching keeps and fixings
  • Wood filler and matching touch-up finish, if required
DIY fallback Reasonable

This is achievable DIY carpentry once the door thickness, construction, backset and existing handle layout are known and compatible privacy-lock sets have been chosen.

Fallback approach

  • Inspect and measure both doors before buying anything, including the door thickness, backset, spindle position and available depth for the lock case.
  • Confirm that neither door is fire-rated unless the chosen hardware and fitting method are approved for that door.
  • Use the supplied template to mark the lock case, spindle and thumb-turn positions, then drill and chisel in small stages.
  • Fit and test each lock repeatedly, including the external emergency release, before making good any redundant holes.

Tools and supplies

  • Tape measure and combination square
  • Drill-driver with suitable wood bits or hole saw
  • Sharp wood chisels and mallet
  • Screwdrivers
  • Lock-fitting jig, if required by the chosen case
  • Eye protection and dust extraction

Stop and hand over if

  • The door construction is unclear or the new case would remove substantial timber.
  • Either door is fire-rated and the proposed hardware or alteration is not approved for it.
  • The latch, thumbturn or emergency release does not operate freely with the door both open and closed.
