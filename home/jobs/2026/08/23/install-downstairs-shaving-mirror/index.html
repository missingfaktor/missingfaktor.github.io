Install shaving mirror beside downstairs mirrored cabinet

Posted on 23 August 2026

Install the purchased Cruze CRZ9958BB illuminated shaving mirror beside the mirrored cabinet above the downstairs sink. It is rechargeable and does not need a mains connection.

1 / 2

Scope:

  • Agree the exact side and height beside the cabinet before drilling.
  • Check that the cabinet doors and the mirror's full folded and extended movement clear each other.
  • Check for concealed pipes and cables, then use fixings suited to the wall.
  • Fit the mirror level and confirm that it moves freely and can be charged in place.

Materials

  • Purchased Cruze CRZ9958BB illuminated shaving mirror
  • Wall fixings suited to the surface
