Install shaving mirror beside downstairs mirrored cabinet

Posted on 23 August 2026

The purchased shaving mirror needs mounting beside the mirrored cabinet above the downstairs sink. It should be reachable while standing at the basin and able to fold, extend and turn without striking the cabinet, its doors or the surrounding wall.

The product is the Cruze Round Illuminated Wall Mounted Cosmetic Mirror, brushed brass, part number CRZ9958BB. It is battery-operated with a built-in rechargeable battery and supplied charging cable, so no mains electrical connection is required.

1 / 2

The product listing records:

  • A 200 mm-diameter round mirror with an approximate overall height of 306 mm.
  • A two-part swivelling arm with an approximate maximum reach of 418 mm from the wall.
  • A wall plate approximately 120 mm high × 64 mm wide.
  • A double-sided mirror with a standard face and 3× magnifying face, with tilt, swivel and 360-degree positioning.
  • A built-in LED rim with touch control for lighting modes and brightness levels.
  • A five-year guarantee. The seller states that all measurements are approximate.

Set out the mounting position before drilling:

  1. Confirm which side of the cabinet gives a useful view at the basin and hold the mirror at a comfortable standing eye height.
  2. Open the cabinet doors and move the shaving mirror through its full folded, extended, tilted and reversed range to confirm that nothing collides.
  3. Keep the charging connection accessible and leave enough space to connect and remove the supplied cable without taking the mirror off the wall.
  4. Identify the wall construction and check for concealed pipes and cables before selecting fixings or making holes.

Fit the wall plate level using fixings suited to the actual substrate and the manufacturer's instructions. If the position is tiled, drill the tile cleanly without hammer action, avoid over-tightening against the tile, and seal the penetrations where the instructions or wall construction require it.

After fitting, test the light controls, both mirror faces, the full arm movement and the cabinet-door clearance.

Materials

  • Purchased Cruze CRZ9958BB illuminated shaving mirror
  • Supplied charging cable
  • Screws and wall plugs or hollow-wall anchors suited to the confirmed substrate
  • Sealant for drilled penetrations, if required by the wall construction or manufacturer
DIY fallback Reasonable

This is a reasonable one-person fitting once the mounting position, wall substrate and absence of concealed services are established. The mirror is rechargeable rather than mains-powered, so the work does not include electrical wiring.

Fallback approach

  • Unpack the mirror, confirm all parts and instructions are present, charge it and test the LED controls and both mirror faces before making holes.
  • Hold it beside the cabinet at a comfortable height, then test the cabinet doors and the mirror's full folded and extended movement before marking the wall plate.
  • Scan the marked area for concealed pipes and cables and identify whether the fixing will enter masonry, solid timber, plasterboard or tile.
  • Drill with a bit and method suited to the surface, fit substrate-appropriate anchors, and fasten the plate level without over-tightening it against tile or plasterboard.
  • Mount the mirror and test the full arm movement, cabinet-door clearance, charging access and light controls.

Tools and supplies

  • Tape measure, pencil and spirit level
  • Pipe and cable detector
  • Drill-driver with bits suited to the confirmed wall surface
  • Screwdrivers
  • Eye protection and dust collection

Stop and hand over if

  • Pipes or cables are detected near the proposed holes, or their route cannot be ruled out.
  • The wall is hollow and a secure load-bearing fixing cannot be identified.
  • A tile is loose, cracks or begins to chip beyond the marked hole.
  • The supplied parts or instructions require a different fixing method from the one planned.
