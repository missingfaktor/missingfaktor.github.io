Supply and fit shutters and blinds

Posted on 2 August 2026

Six openings have no permanent window covering: three bedroom windows, the kitchen bifold doors and two office rooflights. All of them are to be covered in one order, made to measure.

The configuration wanted is:

  • 3x full-height shutters, one to each bedroom window.
  • 1x vertical-blind track across the bifold doors.
  • 2x dimout or translucent blinds for the rooflights.

Everything is to be hand-operated. No motorised, electric or remote-controlled shutters or blinds anywhere, and nothing that needs a battery pack, a charging point or a cable run.

The sketches are mine and the measurements on them are approximate — enough to quote from, not to manufacture from. The appointed supplier carries out the final survey and takes responsibility for the sizes it works to.

Apart from the shutters being wood, materials, fabrics and colours are not settled. These are ballpark quotations: make reasonable assumptions, state what has been assumed, and price on that basis rather than waiting on choices that will be made later.

Master bedroom. A flat three-part window. Panels should follow the existing sections, and both opening casements and their handles must stay usable.

Nursery. A canted three-part bay. Panels and frames must not clash at the two bay corners, and must clear the radiator standing inside the bay.

Guest bedroom. A flat three-part window. Panels follow the existing sections here too, and both handles must stay usable.

All three bedrooms take the same shutter family and the same colour:

  • Full-height plantation shutters in solid hardwood, with a hidden tilt mechanism.
  • Simple frames and matching trims, in the same hardwood as the panels.
  • No PVC, ABS or MDF-cored composite, including anything sold as “faux wood” or “wood-effect”.
  • Independently adjustable upper and lower louvres are wanted, tilted by hand, on a divider rail. Say whether a split tilt is workable on the guest-bedroom window, which is the shortest of the three at roughly 1050 mm.
  • Say which hardwood and what louvre size the price assumes.

The colour is not settled. Bring the full stain and paint range to the survey so it can be chosen against the light in each room, and price on the basis that one colour runs across all three bedrooms.

Bifold doors. One vertical-blind system across the complete opening. The gap between the head of the door frame and the ceiling is tight, and decides whether the track is face-fixed or ceiling-fixed.

Requirements for the bifold blind:

  • Split-centre draw.
  • Fabric that wipes clean, since this is a kitchen. Colour and pattern are open.
  • Wand operation and chainless bottom weights.
  • The doors stack one leaf to the left and three to the right. Neither the track nor the stacked vanes may obstruct the handles or the operation of the leaves.

Office rooflights. Two identical VELUX GPL MK04 top-hung roof windows. Supply two matching blinds compatible with that model. The purpose is glare reduction and daylight control rather than full blackout, so dimout or translucent is right.

Price each opening separately, with the two rooflight blinds as a single line, and say whether measuring, fitting, making good and VAT are included, how many years of warranty each part carries, and roughly when it could be installed.
