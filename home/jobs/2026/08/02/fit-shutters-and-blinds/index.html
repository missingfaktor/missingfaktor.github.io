Supply and fit shutters and blinds

Posted on 2 August 2026

Six openings have no permanent window covering: three bedroom windows, the kitchen bifold doors and two office rooflights. All of them are to be covered in one order, made to measure.

The configuration wanted is:

  • Full-height shutters to all three bedroom windows.
  • One vertical-blind track across the bifold doors.
  • Two manual dimout or translucent blinds for the rooflights.

The measurements below are taken from sketches and are for quotation only. The appointed supplier carries out the final manufacturing survey and takes responsibility for the sizes it works to.

Master bedroom. A flat three-part window, 2230 mm wide × 1240 mm high, in sections of 590, 1050 and 590 mm. The centre section is fixed; both side sections open. Panels should follow the existing sections, and both opening casements and their handles must stay usable.

Nursery. A canted three-part bay, faces of 580, 1680 and 580 mm and 1220 mm high. The centre face is fixed; both side faces open. Shutter panels and frames must not clash at the two bay corners, and a radiator stands below the sill inside the bay.

Guest bedroom. A flat three-part window, 2540 mm wide × 1050 mm high, in sections of 590, 1360 and 590 mm. The centre section is fixed; both side sections open. As in the master bedroom, panels follow the existing sections and both handles must stay usable.

All three bedrooms take the same shutter family and the same colour:

  • Full-height plantation shutters with a hidden tilt mechanism.
  • Simple frames and matching trims.
  • Independently adjustable upper and lower louvres are wanted. The quotation should say whether a split tilt is workable on the 1050 mm guest-bedroom window, which is the shortest of the three.
  • The quotation must state the material, louvre size, warranty and lead time.

The colour is not settled. Bring the full stain and paint range to the survey so it can be chosen against the light in each room, and price on the basis that one colour runs across all three bedrooms.

Bifold doors. One vertical-blind system across the complete opening, 3630 mm wide above the skirting × 2160 mm high. There is roughly 220 mm between the head of the door frame and the ceiling, which decides whether the track is face-fixed or ceiling-fixed.

Requirements for the bifold blind:

  • Split-centre draw.
  • Light-filtering plain fabric in a pale neutral colour.
  • Easy-clean fabric suitable for a kitchen.
  • Wand operation and chainless bottom weights.
  • The doors stack one leaf to the left and three to the right. Neither the track nor the stacked vanes may obstruct the handles or the operation of the leaves.

Office rooflights. Two identical VELUX GPL MK04 top-hung roof windows, nominal size 780 × 980 mm. Supply two matching manual blinds compatible with that model. The purpose is glare reduction and daylight control rather than full blackout, so dimout or translucent is right.

Price the five parts separately:

  1. Master-bedroom shutters.
  2. Nursery bay shutters.
  3. Guest-bedroom shutters.
  4. Bifold vertical blind.
  5. Two rooflight blinds.

State clearly whether the quotation includes:

  • Measuring and fitting.
  • VAT.
  • Making good after fitting.
  • Warranty.
  • An estimated installation date.

The temporary paper blinds will be taken down before fitting, so no removal or disposal needs to be priced.

Materials

  • Full-height plantation shutters with hidden tilt, one colour across three bedrooms
  • Shutter frames and matching trims, including corner posts for the nursery bay
  • One split-centre vertical-blind track and wand-operated vanes for a 3630 mm opening
  • Light-filtering, easy-clean vertical-blind fabric in a pale neutral
  • Two manual dimout or translucent blinds for VELUX GPL MK04 roof windows
DIY fallback Limited

The shutters and the bifold track are made-to-measure work for a fitter. The two rooflight blinds are not: VELUX GPL MK04 blinds are sold to fit that exact model and clip into brackets in the window's own top channel, so they can be ordered direct and fitted from a stepladder.

Fallback approach

  • Confirm the label on both rooflights reads GPL MK04 before ordering, since the blind is sized to the code rather than to a measurement.
  • Order two matching dimout or translucent blinds for that model.
  • Clear the floor below so a stepladder stands level and square to the window.
  • Fit the top brackets into the window's own channel, clip the blind in, and check it runs and holds at every position.

Tools and supplies

  • Stepladder tall enough to reach the head of the rooflight without stretching
  • Screwdriver or cordless driver
  • Tape measure
  • A second adult for ladder safety

Stop and hand over if

  • The label cannot be found or does not read GPL MK04.
  • The rooflight is too high to reach from a stepladder standing on level floor.
  • The top channel is damaged, painted over, or does not take the supplied brackets.
  • Fitting would mean leaning out through an open top-hung window.
